For books in the public domain, try [WikiSource](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Main_Page) or [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org/).

For everything else, in the public domain or otherwise, try [Libby](https://libbyapp.com/). If that's not an option, try these:

- [Rave Book Search](https://ravebooksearch.com/) (VPN or Tor recommended)
- [Anna's Archive](https://annas-archive.gl/) (VPN or Tor recommended; see also: [[notes/how-to-read-books-downloaded-from-annas-archive|How to Read Books Downloaded from Anna's Archive]])
- [Shadow Libraries](https://shadowlibraries.github.io/DirectDownloads/AnnasArchive/) (VPN or Tor recommended)
- [FreeMediaHeckYeah](https://fmhy.net/) (not just books)

[Open Library](https://openlibrary.org/) and [The Internet Archive](https://archive.org/) are decent alternatives, but you generally can't download these books and must read them in the browser. Also, they are often scans of books as opposed to ePub files.
