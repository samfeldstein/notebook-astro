Confidence comes from ability. Ability is the *possession of the means or skill to do something*.[^1]

## Obstacles to confidence

The opposite of confidence is diffidence. Where does diffidence come from? Inhibitions. From *Oxford English*: **A voluntary or involuntary restraint on the direct expression of an instinct.**

[^1]: *New Oxford American English Dictionary*.
