Supposed to be beautiful. Somebody told me there are feral horses just running around.

Home to [[notes/puerto-ricos-bioluminescent-bays|Mosquito Bay]].

You can get there from San Juan by plane or drive to Ceiba and take a ferry. The ferry is much cheaper and the ride isn't too long, but you may have to book ahead of time.

[This Redditor](https://www.reddit.com/r/PuertoRicoTravel/s/4Ic4eUqhDO) recommends renting a jeep.
