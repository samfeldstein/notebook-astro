Supposed to be beautiful. Might be a good spot for a [beach day](https://vieques.com/island-puerto-rico-beaches/). Somebody on Reddit mentioned Cracas Beach. Somebody in person told me there are feral horses just running around.

Home to [[notes/puerto-ricos-bioluminescent-bays|Mosquito Bay]].

You can get there from San Juan by plane or drive to Ceiba and take a ferry. The ferry is much cheaper and the ride isn't too long, but you may have to book ahead of time. I think there's more than one service(maybe?), but [Puerto Rico Ferry](https://www.puertoricoferry.com/) is the one referenced on Vieques's site.

## Car rental

[Maritza's](https://maritzascarrental.com/) says no cars available but their website is spotty so I emailed them.

Segura's is recommended too. I sent them a text.

People seem to get along well with Avis. They have a 4-door jeep for $288 total. Might have to go with that unless we want to wait around forever for these other's to get back to me.

Also texted [VQS Speed Shop](https://www.vqsspeed.shop/). They got back to me right away and will send a quote.

[Vieques UTV Rental](https://viequesutvrental.checkfront.com/reserve/?&action=clr) seems to have plenty of options. Probably the cheapest we'll find. They have a stupid shuttle service though. They say it runs throughout the day but not on an official schedule.
