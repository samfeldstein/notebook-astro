title: How to Clean Cast Iron
private: false
created: 2026-07-23
updated: 2026-07-23
---

1. Rinse under hot water and scrub[^1]
2. Wipe on a thin layer of canola oil[^3]
3. Heat over medium
4. When the oil begins to smoke lightly (about 5 mins), reduce heat and keep it there 5–10 minutes[^2]

[^1]: Nothing too abrasive. Minimal or no soap.

[^2]: I have a coil top so I just turn the stove off and leave the skillet on the burner.

[^3]: Clean microfiber. Paper towel leaves lint.
