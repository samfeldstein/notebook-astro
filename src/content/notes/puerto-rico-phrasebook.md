Oh and! This is a list of some phrases/words we might learn beforehand from Gavin

“Most people speak atleast some english. But some good lines are
 buen provecho - enjoy your food, sort of, good provisions is direct translation 
Permiso- like excuse me, means permission 
Perdón - pardon
Gracias - thank you, pronounce without the s here
Medalla, cerveza de la isla - Medalla, beer of the island.
Calle - Street
Campo - like a mountain town
Puerta - door
Mí mal - my bad
Mí Culpa - my fault
Dónde es el baño - where is the bathroom
Ciudad - city
Qué - what 
Por - for
Con - with
Sin - without
Café - coffee
Azúcar - sugar
Leche - milk
Un café con leche por favor
Yo - I
Tengo - have 
Yo tengo una hora - i have one hour
Tu - you
Tienes - you have 
Qué hora es - what time is it”
