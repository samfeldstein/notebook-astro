Jakub's thoughts are in italic. Not verbatim, of course, but I've done my best to not put words in his mouth. All other words are my extrapolations.

## Ask questions

*If you don't understand an instruction. or a "rule," ask why. If you don't get an answer, you have to find it for yourself.*

We are not beholden to rules or conventions.

He also asks of questions. He often answers a question with a question. For instance, I asked him to help me make my connection less “noisy.” He said, “Do you think your connection is noisy?” He challenges your hypothesis. He wants to know why you think what you think.

## Everything in context

*Everything is a tool, to be used when needed. It is context-dependent; the context being the music and your partner. The reason there are multiple WCS champs is because there isn’t one way to dance the dance. In WCS we create the dance as we dance. The constant in WCS is that it is always danced to contemporary music. The whole concept of WCS is to adapt to the music.*

There are no boxes to tick. There is no Platonic ideal. WCS embraces the [style of no style](https://youtu.be/0yCeduVJW9s?si=-uZ3hyNvc1sIG-rD).

## Stages & updates

*Think of teaching and learning in stages. We might teach a more rigid frame to understand that our feet should move with our hands. But we are not meant to be rigid forever. It is just to teach a principle. Then we update that understanding when we are ready.*

When we update, we keep the principle, but change our physicality to find elasticity instead of rigidity.

## Attend to your connection

*It's not noise in the connection. It's coming to the decision all of a sudden instead of gradually.*

I described my connection as noisy, but he didn't think that was a problem. He demonstrated connection as a stretchy hairband with our fingers hooked through it. You always want some tension in the band, because that tension is how you feel your partner. **Find that tension, and attend to it.**

## Attend to your follow

*Send a clear signal and watch and listen for when it is received. For example, when their foot comes forward, they're ready for the one.*

Don’t do anything until your follow signals they're ready. I've been prioritizing my timing over my follow, so I end up either leading before they're ready or not giving a clear signal before I lead, which is where that all-of-a-suddenness comes from.

## Intent

He mentioned intent at one point, but sort of in passing. I think the idea was that you signal your intent through the tension of the stretch.

## His lead

His lead is soft, and there's a warmth to it. And it's always there. It's reliable.
