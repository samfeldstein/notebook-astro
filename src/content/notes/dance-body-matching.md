Body-matching is a concept in which followers match the tone in their body to what they feel in their arm.

According to [[joseph-demers|Joseph DeMers]], body-matching was first described by [[dax-hock|Dax Hock]]. In his work on [[𝚫pted]], DeMers describes body-matching as an essential component of [[frame-matching]].
