Probability is about counting the number of ways something can happen. The more ways a thing can happen, the more likely it is.

## Calculate probability

*When each outcome is equally likely*, number of successful outcomes divided by the total number of outcomes. For rolling a 1 on a six-sided die: six possible outcomes (rolling a 1-6), one successful outcome (rolling a 1) = $1/6$.
