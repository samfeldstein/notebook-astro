In his *So many gains, so little time*, Andras suggests two days per week:

- Day 1: Hip Hinge, Horizontal Press, Horizontal Row
- Day 2: Hip Hinge (optional), Vertical Push, Vertical Pull

He says any division works, doesn't advise dividing into more than two days a so you have recovery time between training sessions.

You can find the exercises in the [exercise reference](https://www.notion.so/So-Many-Gains-So-Little-Time-Effective-Strength-in-Minimal-Time-Version-1-2-d6a8b7cd00e748dc948b4147cec3b6a7#5b90f0d751bf4e3cbb4918b65be663cf).
