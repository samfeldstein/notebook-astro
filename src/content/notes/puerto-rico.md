## Things to do

Watch the sunset at Punta Higüera.

Tour the Hacienda San Pedro coffee plantation in Jayuya.

Kayak tour in [Mosquito Bay](https://vieques.com/island-bioluminescent-bay/) (bioluminescent).

## Food

**Maví** is a fermented tree-bark juice that tastes something like root beer.
