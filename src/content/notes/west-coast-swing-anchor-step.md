Push with your feet back and up through your ear. When you've anchored, you should be fully sided and your sending leg should be straight with the toe pointed but still connected to the ground.

When you lift your toe, that initiates the lead into the *one*, because it releases the energy you've stored in the stretch. The lead doesn't start on *one*. It starts on the *e-and-a* in *six-e-and-a*.[^1]

[^1]: This was a revelation for me. I always thought my strike was late because I was already moving backward by *one*.
