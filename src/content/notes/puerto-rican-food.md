[Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Puerto_Rico#Places_to_eat) recommends looking for out-of-the-way places. Less expensive + more authentic. Recommends road-side food stands in particular.

## Things to try

**Maví** is a fermented tree-bark juice that tastes something like root beer.

**Mofongo** is fried mashed plantains.
