## Research

[Wikivoyage](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Main_Page) is good for overviews. It also has a travel book feature that compiles and downloads specific pages. You can activate it from the right sidebar, but for some reason I couldn't find that until I went to the [Trip Planner](https://en.wikivoyage.org/wiki/Trip_Planner) page.

An even better way to use it might be with the OsmAnd Maps app. You pay $14.99 a year for a Maps+ subscription and get to download the entire Wikivoyage site for use in the app. It also lets you download Wikipedia articles related to whatever region and marks related points of interest.

I haven't looked at *Lonely Planet* books yet but I plan to.

## Trip-planning software

Google Calendar should do it. You can add notes, link to things, and add collaborators.

I also mark points of interest on Google Maps.

OsmAnd Maps is handy for reasons stated above.
