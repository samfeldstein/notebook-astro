Bring your weight forward on your ankles until your toes engage.

Then push your hips back and until your [chest is over your toes](https://youtube.com/shorts/H6cd_KemBaE?si=XJgfND6KFQiFueVY).

I've heard the hip-hinge method (as opposed to pushing your chest forward) expressed by Thibault and Nicole and a tango instructor in Denver.
