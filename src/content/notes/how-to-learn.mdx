Learning to learn is pointless. Learning is a means. You need an end. If your end is to become a better dancer, and you devote some of your life to achieving that end, you will immerse yourself in that world and the learning will happen on its own.

[[learning-by-project|Learn by project]].

## When stuck

<BlockQuote cite="https://www.highagency.com/" author="George Mack" medium="essay" work="High Agency in 30 Minutes">
Find the simple ideas via inversion. Flip the problem around. And take them seriously before adding any complexity.

E.g. "I want to become a better writer”

The midwit will start learning about writers' coffee-drinking routines or researching the perfect keyboard to enhance their word count. Instead, how would you ensure you become a worse writer?

1.  Do not write
2.  Write inconsistently
3.  Write about things you find boring

Flip them around and you've found the simple ideas the guy on the left will come up with:

1.  Write
2.  Write consistently
3.  Write about things that excite you
</BlockQuote>
